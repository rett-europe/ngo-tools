Budget scenario explorer

Plan is the current budget: each category's cost lines at their planned driver counts (or the amount you type for a category without lines), plus the default contingency. Change is a percentage on a category, on top of the driver levers and the price change; the Scenario box shows the result. Type an exact Scenario amount instead and Change shows the percentage it implies. Contingency is then added.

Edit cost lines and drivers

The coloured bar is the expected funding by source. The black line marks the cost. A bar that stops short of the line leaves a gap paid from reserves; a bar past the line is a surplus.

Start with the day-to-day running result, take away the event costs, then add each source of event funding. The last bar is the result.
