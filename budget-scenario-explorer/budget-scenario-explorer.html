Budget scenario explorer

Everything on this tab is an absolute amount saved in the file: the budget as you know it today. It is the Plan that the New edition tab starts from.

Plan comes from the Baseline tab: each category's cost lines at their planned driver counts (or its lump sum), plus the planned contingency. Change is a percentage on a category, on top of the driver levers and the price change; the New edition box shows the result. Type an exact New edition amount instead and Change shows the percentage it implies. Contingency is then added.

The coloured bar is the expected funding by source. The black line marks the cost. A bar that stops short of the line leaves a gap paid from reserves; a bar past the line is a surplus.

Start with the day-to-day running result, take away the event costs, then add each source of event funding. The last bar is the result.
